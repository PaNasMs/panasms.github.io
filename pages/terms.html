Self-hosted operation

You operate your own NAS and are responsible for selecting permitted users, securing its credentials and network access, installing trusted modules, maintaining backups and using cloud accounts and files that you are authorized to access. Cloud synchronization can overwrite or delete files according to the task settings; use suitable backups and review two-way synchronization settings before enabling them.
